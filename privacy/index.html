Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Shamy is an iPhone app that locks your social apps and lets you open them when you get your tasks done. It's built so your data stays on your iPhone.

What we don't do

  • We have no accounts and no servers of our own: you don't need to sign up.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • We don't use ads or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the app work and where people get stuck, Shamy sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service with servers in the European Union that complies with the GDPR.

  • What is sent: which setup screens are shown and for how long, which options you pick in its questions (for example "read" or "before bed"), whether you grant the Screen Time permission, how many apps you pick (just the number), buttons like "buy" or "continue for free", and things like completing a task or starting a focus session (without its text). Also general technical data: app and iOS version, iPhone model and language.
  • What is never sent: your name, the text of your tasks or routines, which apps or websites you lock, your Screen Time data or any other text you type.
  • Each iPhone is counted with an anonymous identifier that is hashed on the iPhone itself before it leaves. Neither we nor TelemetryDeck can tell whose it is or link it to you.
  • It isn't used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites, and it isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

Screen Time

Shamy uses Apple's Screen Time permission (Family Controls) to lock the apps you pick and show you how much you use them. Apple doesn't let that data leave your iPhone: not even we can see it.

What's stored on your iPhone

Your tasks, routines, settings, your name (if you type it) and your answers during setup are stored only on your iPhone (for the answers, only the chosen option is included in the anonymous statistics above). Deleting the app deletes them.

Voice

If you add tasks by speaking, Shamy uses Apple's speech recognition. Depending on your iPhone, Apple may process the audio on its servers to turn it into text, under its own privacy policy. Shamy doesn't store the audio.

Saved videos

If you share a link with Shamy, the app asks the original website for that link's title and image, directly from your iPhone, to show it in your list.

Purchases

The Shamy Plus subscription is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details; we only know, on your iPhone, whether the subscription is active.

Children

Shamy is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't collect personal data from anyone.
