FAQ

My apps aren't locking

Shamy needs the Screen Time permission. Open Shamy's Settings and check that the permission is granted and that you've picked your apps.

How many apps can I lock for free?

One for free. With Shamy Plus, as many as you want.

How do I cancel Shamy Plus?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Shamy. In the app: Settings › Shamy Plus › Manage.

I switched iPhones and don't have Plus

In Shamy's Settings, tap “Restore purchases” with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
